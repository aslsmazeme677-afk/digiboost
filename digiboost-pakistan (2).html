Quick Wins

Top 5 Digital Marketing Tips

Implement these today and see results within weeks.

01

Optimize Your Google Business Profile

A complete Google Business listing brings free, high-intent local customers every day — without spending a single rupee on ads.

02

Post Consistently on 1 Social Platform First

Master one channel before spreading thin. Post quality content 3–5x per week on the platform where your audience lives.

03

Use WhatsApp for Customer Retention

WhatsApp has 98% open rates. Build a broadcast list and send offers, updates & tips to keep customers coming back.

04

Create Short Video Content

Reels and TikToks under 60 seconds get 3x the reach of static posts. Show your product or expertise in action.

05

Collect & Display Customer Reviews

Social proof builds trust instantly. One good Google review can close 10 new sales — ask every happy customer!
